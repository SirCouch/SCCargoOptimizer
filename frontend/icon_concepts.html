App Icon Concepts

Five directions for the SCCargoOptimizer application icon. Each is a standalone SVG in frontend/icon_concepts/, drawn on a 256×256 rounded plate with the same deep-space background and cyan/orange accent palette so they can be compared like-for-like. Each row shows the icon at desktop, taskbar, and tray sizes — the 32px and 16px cells are the ones that matter for the Windows build. Once a direction is picked, it gets exported to a multi-resolution .ico and wired into app.spec.

A

Stacked Hold

A tidy isometric stack of mixed-size crates on a glowing cargo-grid pad — the app's 3D viewer in miniature. Orange/cyan/slate crates echo the in-app box textures. Strong, chunky silhouette that survives 16px well.

Last Box In

The optimizer's whole job in one frame: a holographic wireframe hold, nearly packed, with the final crate dropping into the one cell that fits (dashed target on the floor). The most literal "packing solved" story; the wireframe softens at very small sizes.

SCU Crate

A single front-facing SCU crate as a badge: armored panel face, vents, glowing cyan latch and status strip, orange corner clamps. Flat and bold — the most conventional "app icon" of the set and the most legible at tray size.

Neural Cargo

A cube drawn as a graph — cyan nodes on every vertex, one orange decision node in the middle with dotted attention arcs. A nod to the GAT ensemble doing the packing. Most distinctive silhouette in a taskbar full of boxes; least literal about cargo.

Manifest Grid

Top-down cargo grid with tetris-fit blocks and one remaining slot highlighted as the optimal placement (dashed cyan, plus mark). Reads as "optimization" instantly, stays crisp at every size, and mirrors the manifest/grid UI of the app itself.
